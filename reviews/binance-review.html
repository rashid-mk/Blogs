Frequently Asked Questions

Is Binance the biggest crypto exchange?
Yes. Binance consistently ranks as the world's largest crypto exchange by trading volume, typically handling $50–80B in daily spot and derivatives volume. It's the most liquid exchange globally for major crypto pairs.
What happened in the Binance 2019 hack?
In May 2019, hackers stole approximately $40M (7,000 BTC) from Binance's hot wallet. Binance covered all losses using its SAFU fund — no user lost any funds. Since then, Binance has significantly overhauled its security infrastructure.
Can US residents use Binance?
US residents cannot use Binance.com. Binance.US is a separate, regulated entity available for US users but offers a significantly reduced feature set and coin selection compared to the global platform.
How do I reduce fees on Binance?
The easiest way is to use BNB (Binance Coin) to pay for trading fees, which grants a 25% discount (0.075% from 0.1%). Additional discounts come from increasing your 30-day trading volume to reach higher VIP tiers.
What earn products does Binance offer?
Binance offers Simple Earn (flexible and locked staking up to 20% APY), Dual Investment, Liquid Swap (AMM pools), ETH staking, and Launchpad for early-access token sales. It's the most comprehensive earn ecosystem of any CEX.
