Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Bitget's copy trading unique?
Bitget has the world's largest copy trading network with 80,000+ verified professional traders. You can filter traders by win rate, total returns, risk level, number of followers, and trading style. All traders are verified and their historical performance is transparent and auditable.
Is Bitget safe?
Yes. Bitget has never experienced a major hack and maintains a $300M protection fund — the largest in the industry. All user funds are held in cold storage with multi-signature security. Regular third-party Proof-of-Reserves audits confirm full backing of assets.
How much do I need to start copy trading on Bitget?
You can start copy trading on Bitget with as little as $10. The platform automatically allocates your funds proportionally to the trades made by the trader you're copying. There's no fixed minimum for the trader's profit share — you only pay 10% of your profits.
Does Bitget accept US users?
No, Bitget is not available to US residents. US traders should look at Coinbase, Kraken, or Gemini as regulated alternatives.
What is BGB and how does it reduce fees?
BGB is Bitget's native exchange token. Holding and using BGB to pay trading fees provides a 20% discount on all spot trading fees, reducing the effective rate from 0.10% to 0.08%.
