Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bybit safe to use?
Yes. Bybit has operated since 2018 without a major security incident. It uses 100% cold storage, publishes quarterly Proof-of-Reserves, and maintains a large insurance fund. It is one of the safest exchanges available.
Does Bybit accept US users?
No. Bybit is not available to US residents due to regulatory restrictions. US-based traders should consider exchanges like Coinbase or Kraken.
What is the minimum deposit on Bybit?
There is no minimum deposit for crypto deposits. For fiat purchases via card or bank transfer, the minimum is typically $10–$20 depending on the payment provider.
How does Bybit compare to Binance?
Bybit beats Binance on futures fees (0.01% vs 0.02% maker), customer support quality (24/7 live chat vs mostly bots), and interface cleanliness. Binance wins on coin variety and spot volume. For derivatives trading, Bybit is the clear winner.
Does Bybit require KYC verification?
KYC is optional for basic trading (with withdrawal limits). Full KYC unlocks higher withdrawal limits, fiat deposits, and all platform features. The process typically takes 5–15 minutes.
What cryptocurrencies can I trade on Bybit?
Bybit offers 300+ spot trading pairs and 200+ perpetual futures contracts. Major coins include BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, ADA, DOGE, and many altcoins and DeFi tokens.
