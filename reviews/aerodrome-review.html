Aerodrome Review 2026

★★★★★ 4.7 / 5 Based on 150+ hours of testing
✈️ Base #1 DEX 📊 Base L2 Liquidity Hub 🌐 Base (Coinbase L2)

Aerodrome Finance is the central trading and liquidity hub of the Base blockchain (Coinbase's Layer 2). Commanding over 50% of the entire TVL on Base, Aerodrome utilizes powerful ve-tokenomics (veAERO) to incentivize deep liquidity for all Base ecosystem tokens.

Last updated: August 15, 2026 · Reviewed by Alex Rivera

⚡ Bottom Line

Aerodrome is the #1 decentralized exchange on Base. With pennies in gas fees, deep Coinbase ecosystem liquidity, and high staking yields, it is the cornerstone of the booming Base network.

🔍 veAERO Flywheel & Base Liquidity Engine

Modeled after Velodrome on Optimism, Aerodrome locks 100% of protocol fees to veAERO voters who direct token emissions to the most productive pools.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I trade on Aerodrome?
Connect any Web3 wallet (Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, Rabby) switched to the Base network, and swap instantly with near-zero gas fees.
What is veAERO?
veAERO is vote-escrowed AERO token obtained by locking AERO. Voters earn 100% of swap fees generated by the pools they vote for.
Is Aerodrome safe and legitimate?
Yes. Aerodrome is 100% non-custodial and governed decentralized on-chain by veAERO token lockers.
How do I get started on Aerodrome?
Visit the official Aerodrome website, create your account, enable Two-Factor Authentication, deposit funds via crypto or supported fiat payment methods, and you can begin trading immediately.
