How We Rate

Our Scoring Methodology

Every exchange is evaluated across 50+ individual data points grouped into six core categories. Each category is weighted by its relative importance to the average trader.

💰 Fees & Costs 25%

Spot maker/taker fees, futures fees, withdrawal fees, deposit fees, spread analysis, and VIP tier accessibility. We calculate the true all-in cost of a $1,000 trade.

🔒 Security 25%

Cold storage ratio, 2FA options, Proof-of-Reserves audit quality, insurance fund size, hack history, and withdrawal security features.

🖥️ User Experience 20%

Web interface clarity, mobile app quality, order execution speed, advanced charting tools, and beginner onboarding quality.

📊 Trading Features 15%

Spot, margin, futures, options, copy trading, earn products, API quality, and coin/pair selection breadth.

🎧 Customer Support 10%

Response times, support channel availability (live chat, email, phone), quality of resolution, and availability in multiple languages.

⚖️ Regulatory Standing 5%

Licenses held, jurisdictions served, compliance history, and regulatory actions or sanctions.

Our Testing Process — Step by Step

1

Account Creation

We open real accounts, time the KYC process, and document any friction in the onboarding experience.

2

Fund Deposit

We deposit funds via multiple methods (crypto, bank transfer, card) and record deposit times and any fees.

3

Live Trading

We execute real trades on spot, futures, and margin markets, recording execution quality, slippage, and actual fees paid.

4

Support Testing

We submit 5+ support tickets per exchange and document response times, quality, and resolution rates.

5

Withdrawal Testing

We withdraw funds in multiple cryptocurrencies and record processing times and actual withdrawal fees.

6

Security Audit

We independently verify Proof-of-Reserves, review third-party audit reports, and assess security infrastructure.

7

Final Scoring & Review

All data is compiled, weighted scores calculated, and a final editorial review is completed by a senior analyst before publication.
