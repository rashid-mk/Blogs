Can You Trade Crypto Futures Without KYC in 2026? (The Honest Answer)

The Short Answer

Technically, yes — limited crypto futures trading without full KYC is still possible on decentralized platforms (like dYdX or GMX) in 2026. However, on centralized exchanges, no-KYC futures trading is effectively dead. Major platforms gate derivatives behind mandatory identity verification.

What Does "No KYC" Actually Mean?

Exchanges operate on a spectrum of verification. Email-only accounts offer view-only access or minimal spot functionality, whereas Level 2 Full KYC unlocks high-leverage futures, large withdrawals, and account recovery options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a VPN to trade on Bybit from the US?

Using a VPN to access an exchange from a restricted jurisdiction violates the platform's Terms of Service and may lead to account suspension and loss of funds.

What happens if I refuse KYC after depositing?

If you refuse KYC when prompted, your account will be frozen until you complete identity verification.

Is no-KYC trading illegal?

Trading itself is not illegal, but evading tax reporting or regulatory rules in your local jurisdiction carries serious penalties.
