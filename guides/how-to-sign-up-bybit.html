Setting up a new account takes less than 5 minutes. Follow this step-by-step tutorial to register safely and claim your initial welcome bonuses.

Step 2

Enter Email & Create a Strong Password

Choose between registering via email address or mobile phone number. Enter a strong password containing:

  • At least 8-30 characters long
  • At least one uppercase letter and one lowercase letter
  • At least one number and special character (@, #, $, etc.)

Click "Get My Welcome Gifts" to proceed.

Step 3

Verify Email Verification Code

Bybit will immediately send a 6-digit verification code to your email or SMS. Open your inbox, copy the code, and enter it into the registration window within 60 seconds.

Step 4

Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Before depositing any funds, secure your account by navigating to Account & Security and binding Google Authenticator (2FA). Store your 16-digit secret key on paper in a safe location.

Step 5

Complete Level 1 Identity Verification (KYC)

To unlock spot trading, futures, and deposit options, complete Level 1 KYC by submitting a government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID, or driver's license) and a quick facial selfie scan. Approval usually takes under 3 minutes.
