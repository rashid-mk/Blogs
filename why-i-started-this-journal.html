Why I started this journal

Most trading content is sold after the fact. This one gets written before the outcome is known — that's the whole point.

I work in IT support during the day. Trading is what I do on the side, seriously, on my own time — crypto futures using Smart Money Concepts, and gold and silver alongside it. This site is where I write it down before I know if I was right.

Most trading content online works backwards. Someone posts a chart after the trade already worked, draws the zone in hindsight, and calls it a strategy. It's easy to look sharp when you already know the answer. I wanted the opposite of that — a record that can't be edited after the fact, where the reasoning, the structure read, and the level that proves me wrong are all written down while the trade is still open.

The entry is only useful if it was true before the candle closed — not after.

So here's what this journal actually is: real setups across BTC, ETH, and other pairs I trade on Bitunix, plus XAU/USD and XAG/USD reads using the same Smart Money framework — CHoCH, BOS, fair value gaps, demand and supply zones. Each entry marks the invalidation level up front. No signals for sale, no cropped screenshots of only the wins. If a setup doesn't work, that entry stays up exactly as written.

I'm not trading full-time yet, and I'm not claiming to have this fully figured out. This is a proving window — keeping the day job in IT while building an honest track record on the side. If that's useful to anyone else thinking about the same path, that's a welcome side effect. Mostly, I'm writing this for the version of me six months from now, to see if the reasoning actually held up.
